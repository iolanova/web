How can I organize gauge swatches, color schemes, and patterns across multiple projects so that I can use them for new projects and quickly find everything whenever I need it?

All your items – such as gauge swatches, color schemes, and patterns – are saved in lists that you can access through the Organizer. For example, if you have an idea for a pattern, you can create the pattern first and later, when you have an idea for a project, add that pattern to the new project.

How can I quickly find specific list entries and my favorites?

You can mark list entries as favorites in edit mode using the heart-button. In the list overview, you can filter your favorites using the heart-button in the top menu. You can also sort entries directly using drag-and-drop to arrange them however you like.
The app retains your chosen sort order even after you close it. This way, you can always keep track of everything with your personalized layout. If you ever need to search for something, just use the magnifying-glass-button for a full-text search.

Can I delete list entries?

Yes, if you swipe the entry to the left, you'll see a button to delete it. If you've linked the item to a project, you'll see a warning before deleting it.

Can I use multiple counters in the project at the same time??

A set of counters is already predefined for each project type. When you create a new project, you can use these counters right away, since they adjust to your measurements and your project’s gauge swatch. All counters in the project retain their status when you switch between them. In the atelier, you can quickly and easily switch back and forth between counters with a swipe gesture.
In addition to the pre-set counters, you can scroll to the “Belonging Counters” section in the project’s Edit mode. There, you can use the plus-button to add more counters and use them in your project.

How do I know when I've reached a row in my knitting where I need to make an adjustment?

The built-in counters already include all the instructions you need for tricky parts of your project, such as when to start decreasing for the yoke or when your sleeves should reach the desired circumference. These instructions appear automatically when the counter reaches the row calculated for that step.

How can I create additional counters?

In addition to the pre-set counters, you can scroll to the “Belonging Counters” section in the project’s edit mode. There, you can use the plus-button to add more counters and use them in your project. When creating a counter, you’ll immediately see the total height as you enter your target rows. That’s possible because these counters are also linked to your gauge swatch.

Can I adjust or reset counters?

Yes, you can reset the entire counter directly within the counter using the delete-button (on the left), or adjust it one step at time using the adjust-button (on the right).

How can I add notes and reminders to my knitting projects?

There is a note field available for each of your projects. You have two options for entering or editing a note in your project: You can click the note-button in the top-left menu directly in the atelier. This will open a sheet where you can enter and save your belonging notes. Alternatively, you can click “Edit” in your project view and in the “Notes” section you can enter your personal experiences or important information.

How can I quickly delete the content of my notes?

Once you've opened the note field, you can use the eraser-button to quickly and easily delete the entire entry. If you accidentally clicked the button, simply cancel the action without saving and start again.

How can I pick up right where I left off after taking a break from knitting?

Knitting Suite doesn’t require markers. The app automatically saves your last status (e.g., the counter readings) when you exit or close the app. For example, if you’re knitting the pattern for your left sleeve and you’re currently on row 6 of 20, the counter will remember this exact position and start from the same spot the next time you open this pattern counter.
You don’t even have to continue directly with the left sleeve for your next knitting session. You can switch counters and, for example, knit the right sleeve up to the same point before returning to the left sleeve. It’s entirely up to you what you want to knit, when, and how far you want to continue.

How can I add a knitting project to the atelier or switch between projects?

Add your current project to the atelier by first tapping the bookmark-button in the top menu bar and then selecting the project you want to knit. Then confirm your selection by tapping the bookmark-button again. The project will then appear in the atelier. This way, as soon as you open the atelier, you’ll have an overview of your project, along with all related counters and notes, right at your fingertips. To switch projects, simply tap the bookmark-button in the top menu again and select a different project. When you confirm again, Knitting Suite will swap the project in the atelier.
If you ever forget to confirm, don’t worry – you just won’t be able to navigate back to your project view. Simply confirm your selection, and everything will work again.

Help! I can't select a project from the project list anymore!

You probably still have the bookmark-button active. Click on it again so that the list regains its lightly colored background; then you should be able to navigate back to your project view.

Can I turn off or control the animations?

In the atelier, you'll find the play/stop-button for the animations in the lower-left corner. Turn it on or off as desired.
Attention: Turning it on again will suggest a different animation; if you're looking for a specific one, click several times until the animation appears.

How can I prevent the screen from going into sleep mode while knitting with Knitting Suite?

Use the eye-open-button in the lower-left corner in the atelier; this will keep sleep mode disabled until you click the button again

Can I rate my knitting projects and record my personal experiences?

Yes, using a selectable emoji icon in edit-project-mode. With the emoji you can indicate whether your project was challenging, agonizing, or something you love.
You can also use the note field to jot down your own thoughts and tips. This way, all important information is saved within the project.
Extra tip: You can use emojis directly within a note.

Can I report a problem, give feedback, or suggest a new feature for the app?

We’d be thrilled to receive your feedback or, for example, suggestions for new features. So don’t hesitate to send us a great idea or some (hopefully positive) feedback. In the app settings you’ll find a “contact & feedback” section.




Always make your gauge swatch first

The gauge swatch determines your knitting style. For example, if you’ve knitted 20 rows of 20 stitches and enter the measured height and width of this swatch into the app, everything else can be calculated.
Only once your gauge swatch is saved will the counters be adjusted to your measurements.

Calculate your yarn usage precisely

Place your gauge swatch on a scale without the needles and enter the measured weight into the app.
The app will then show how much yarn you need for your project. (To be on the safe side, just use one more ball than indicated.)

Use up your leftover yarn

If you have yarn left over from your last project, you can check which projects you can still make.
By entering the weight of your gauge swatch, the app will show you how much yarn you’ll need. Then weigh your leftover yarn and compare that amount to the amount displayed in the app.

Make active use of your notes!

Write down in the “Notes” section how many rows of cuff you knit for the first sleeve and how many rows before you start the pattern; that way, you can knit the same number of rows for the second sleeve without having to count beforehand.

Color multiple fields at once while creating your patterns

When creating a pattern, click the brush-button in the lower-left corner. Once you’re in brush mode, you can color multiple fields in your pattern by brushing over them.
Scrolling is temporarily disabled so that you don’t move the pattern while you’re coloring it. If you turn off this multi-selection coloring mode, you can scroll sideways again and move your pattern back and forth.

Use the selectable standard sizes as a starting point

The size buttons next to the schematic drawing of a new project (in the dialog box for entering measurements) provide default values that you can then adjust to your liking. For example, click on size S and then reduce the length of the knit sweater to suit your own style.

Customize the app through the settings

As you may have noticed, you can configure the appearance and language (English or German) on the settings page.
Further you can change your name for the welcome message here.
You can also change the default visibility of the patterns in the pattern counter or adjust the settings to keep the screen awake in the atelier and in the counters.
