Unlock the Secrets of Economics with CauseffEcon – Your Ultimate Learning Companion!

Explore cause-and-effect-chains in economics, deepen your understanding of global events. Set personal goals and track your progress!

Economics is all around us. A seemingly simple change in a government policy, market fluctuation, or international trade decision can have far-reaching consequences that impact businesses, consumers, and entire economies. From everyday transactions to global financial events, understanding the intricate web of cause-and-effect relationships is key.

Ready?

If you’re ready to explore the fascinating world of economics, CauseffEcon is the perfect app for you. No user tracking, no 3rd party links, no ads, no costs – just play. Simply download and start your journey to mastering cause-and-effect-chains in economics with CauseffEcon—your future self will thank you for it!


Whether you're a student preparing for exams, a professional, or simply curious about how the economic world operates – CauseffEcon is the app to deepen your understanding of the interconnected world of economics.

Students & Learners: This app focuses on helping you preparing for exams, seminars or oral discussions. It will sharpen your cause-and-effect reasoning skills and train a more nuanced and in-depth grasp of economic processes. Whether you’re a high school student or working on advanced coursework, or for the International Baccalaureate (IB), you’ll find the app’s quizzes to be a valuable resource for exam preparation.
By engaging with the quiz levels, you’ll notice, that your responses move beyond basic facts. Instead, you’ll begin to think critically about the underlying mechanisms driving economic events. With each successful quiz, your ability to anticipate consequences, understand market behavior, and connect economic phenomena improves.

Economists & managers: Even beyond education, CauseffEcon is valuable for anyone interested in economics. Training helps practitioners, business owners, and managers to better understand how economic forces shape markets, and industries. For example, when you hear about a government’s fiscal policy or an economic downturn, you’ll be challenged to understand what’s at stake, how it affects different sectors, and what consequences may arise in the future.


Learn at your own pace:
No one is judging you. CauseffEcon is designed to provide a dynamic and engaging learning environment: - Set your own targets to reach and adjust the difficulty to suit your skills. - Choose the ratio of correct and incorrect answers to challenge but not overwhelm you. - Decide whether you’re in "Train Mode" for ongoing practice or "Exam Mode" to simulate a test-like experience.

Choose difficulty levels:

1. Rookie Level – Ideal for beginners. At this level, you’ll cover the basics of economics, including:
- Economic Systems
- Pricing Policies
- Market Failures
- …

2. Pro Level – For those who want to dive deeper into more advanced topics such as:
- Economic Growth & Employment
- Demand and Supply-Oriented Economic Policies
- External Balance & Environmental Issues
- …

3. Expert Level – Get a real-world perspective on global economic events. This level explores actual cases and their ripple effects, giving you the complexities of economics in practice. Choose from topics like:
- World Bank Report 2024
- Financial & Economic Crisis 2008/09
- Impact of Post-COVID-19 De-Globalization
- ...

Content Help: If you get stuck, you can refer to detailed content help that explains the basics and background to the economic event. This ensures that you’re not just memorizing answers but actually understand the deeper connections and mechanisms at play.
Track your progress and celebrate goals: We know that staying motivated is key to long-term learning. That’s why CauseffEcon includes features to track your progress.

Observe how your knowledge grows!

Turn your fruit into delicious, perfectly set jam – without stress and without mess.

Jam Calculator is your all-round helper for easy and precise jam making without leftovers. Whether you want to preserve your summer harvest or use up overripe fruit, the Jam Calculator shows you exactly what you need, instantly. Perfect, even if you just want a single jar of sweet marmelade!

Download Jam Calculator and bring calm, precision, and a little sweetness to your kitchen—no leftovers, perfect flavor, and deliciously filled jars.

Why a Jam Calculator app?

Say goodbye to guesswork—making jam at home shouldn't be confusing, but fun! But between calculating sugar proportions, gelling agent amounts, and jar sizes, even experienced mental math pros can get confused.
With Jam Calculator, figuring out the quantities is as easy as picking low hanging fruits. Whether it's strawberry jam for the family or apricot jam for the winter, Jam Calculator does the math so you can focus on the flavor. No more scribbled notes or sticky fingers on calculator buttons!

Jam Calculator automatically calculates the ideal quantities for:
• Fruit
• Jam sugar or regular sugar
• Gelling agents (pectin, jam sugar, etc.)
• Required jars
Simply enter the desired amount of fruit—or alternatively the amount of jam sugar or gelling agent—and Jam Calculator will show you what you need. Jam Calculator supports different ratios for low-sugar jam or traditional recipes.

Designed for real kitchens – From plums to sour cherries, grapes, and oranges – no matter what fruit you use, Jam Calculator ensures that nothing goes to waste and every jar is filled – whether it's one jar or many.


Who is this app ideal for?

For leftover avoiders – never again will you have to throw away that last bowl of berries or wonder what to do with an opened package of jam sugar.
Simply enter your remaining quantity and the app will show you what you need to add.

For stockpiling enthusiasts – large quantities or lots of jars? No problem with Jam Calculator. The jam app calculates exactly how many jars you need for each quantity, based on your chosen jar size (from 30 ml to 500 ml) and your input. The total quantity is calculated immediately and the jars you need are displayed.

For variety artists – Want to try out different flavors with your sugar packet or just make a small test batch? The Jam Calculator figures out the right mix. Simple, fast and easy peasy.

Ideal for:
• Children who want to learn with their parents how to make jam
• Experienced hobby jam makers who want to speed up the process
• Gardeners who want to preserve their seasonal fruits, even when the harvest varies.
• Hobby cooks, and households who hate waste and love flavor


Features:
• Intuitive user interface – no additional knowledge required
• Text input or slider, choose whatever you prefer
• Simple button selection of the gelling ratio from 1:1 to 4:1 – even after entering the quantity
• Easy switching between fruit or jam sugar input, easy switching between jam sugar and gelfix/pectin
• Add liquids (wine, water, etc.) and the calculation will be adjusted accordingly
• Large result display so you can see everything at a glance, even when you're busy cooking
• Dark and light mode
• Various languages available: German, English
• Quick and easy – no complicated menu navigation
• Works even without the Internet
• No registration or entry of personal data required

Less calculating. More jam!

Why a Baking Studio app?

The Baking Studio app brings structure, creativity, and confidence to an activity that many people love but often find challenging. It provides users with a space where they can learn, experiment, and develop without pressure—combining guidance with creativity in a way that traditional baking books can not.
With customizable ingredients, step-by-step instructions, and a built-in timer, the Baking Studio app empowers everyone to bake with confidence, discover their personal style, and enjoy the process from start to finish.


Who is this app ideal for?

• Parents who want to bake with their children in a playful and creative way.
• Home bakeries who want to try new combinations beyond the strict instructions of recipes, without straying too far from the essential basic combinations.
• Creative bakers who like to experiment and develop their own recipes.
• Beginners who like to express themselves within a ‘framework’ so as not to feel overwhelmed.
• Leftover avoiders who want smart suggestions on how to use remaining ingredients efficiently.
• Stockpiling enthusiasts who keep well-filled pantries and want recipe ideas based on what they already have.
• Busy people who appreciate tools such as timers and step-by-step instructions alongside their creative combinations.
• Foodies who are looking for a fun, inspiring way to improve their baking skills and are brave enough to try experimental recipes.
• Content creators or hobby bakers who like to document and improve their baking skills.

In short, it’s perfect for anyone who wants to bake more confidently, creatively, and resourcefully.

• Intuitive user interface – no additional knowledge required
• Modular recipe generator – Combine, mix, and match baking ingredients to create your own favorite recipes.
